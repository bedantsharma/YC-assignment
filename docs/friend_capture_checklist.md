# Capture checklist (iPhone 16 Pro)

Total time: about 1.5 hours. Do it in daylight with all room lights on.

## Before you start

- [ ] Install **Stray Scanner** (free) and **Polycam** (free) from the App Store
- [ ] Phone charged, at least 15 GB free
- [ ] Tape measure (a laser measurer is even better), masking tape, a pen, a sheet of paper
- [ ] Pick **3 or more rooms plus the hallway/corridor that connects them**
- [ ] Pick **one furnished room** for fake damage (nothing permanent):
  - **Crack:** a strip of masking tape on a wall with a thin dark line drawn along it (~40 cm)
  - **Water stain:** a sheet of paper stained with tea or coffee, taped high on a wall or
    near a ceiling corner
- [ ] Draw a rough sketch of the home on paper. Number the rooms. In each room, label the
      walls A, B, C... clockwise starting from the door

## 1. LiDAR scans (Stray Scanner)

- [ ] **Whole home, one recording:** start in the hallway, walk through every room, and end
      back where you started. Phone at chest height, walk slowly, sweep each wall top to
      bottom, point at the ceiling for 2–3 seconds in every room, and look straight at every
      door and window frame. About 3–5 minutes
- [ ] **Damaged room, recording 1:** just that room, about 1 minute
- [ ] **Damaged room, recording 2:** the same room again, a separate recording

## 2. Video (normal Camera app)

Settings: Video, 1x lens (not 0.5x), no Cinematic or Action mode.

- [ ] **Whole home:** the same walk as the LiDAR scan, same pace
- [ ] **Damaged room:** twice, as two separate videos

## 3. Photos (normal Camera app)

Settings: Photo, 1x lens, no Portrait mode, no zoom.

- [ ] For **each room**, 4–8 photos: one from each corner looking across the room, plus
      the walls the corners miss
- [ ] In **every doorway**, one photo looking **into the next room** (this lets the rooms be
      joined together)
- [ ] **Damaged room:** a second full set of photos
- [ ] Note which photos belong to which room (or make one album per room)

## 4. Polycam (free tier)

- [ ] Scan **two rooms** (the damaged room plus one other) in **Room** mode
- [ ] Export whatever the free tier allows, and screenshot the floor plan showing the
      dimensions

## 5. Measurements (the most important part)

Write everything on the sketch, in centimetres with one decimal place if you can (e.g. 342.5).

- [ ] Every **wall length**, corner to corner, at about waist height
- [ ] **Ceiling height** in every room, measured in 2 different spots
- [ ] Every **door and window width**, inside the frame, plus the height
- [ ] The **size of each fake damage** (crack length; stain width x height)
- [ ] Which rooms connect to which, through which door
- [ ] Take a clear photo of the finished sketch

## Avoid

- People or pets walking through the shot
- Moving fast or spinning on the spot
- Standing directly in front of mirrors
- Turning lights off partway through

## Sending the files

- **Do not use WhatsApp:** it compresses photos and videos
- Stray Scanner: Files app → On My iPhone → Stray Scanner → zip each recording folder
- Upload everything (Stray Scanner zips, original videos, original photos, Polycam export,
  sketch photo) to **Google Drive or WeTransfer** and share the link
