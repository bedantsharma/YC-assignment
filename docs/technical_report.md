# Technical report: phone captures to a measured floor plan

Cozmo AI "Applied AI" take-home. Every number here is generated by a script in this repo
(named next to it) and comes from [benchmark_report.md](benchmark_report.md) unless stated;
`scripts/reproduce.sh` regenerates all of them from the raw captures.

## 1. What it does

`uv run floorplan run <capture>` turns one capture into `plan.json` and `plan.png`: rooms
(rectilinear outlines), wall lengths, floor area, ceiling height, doors and passages,
adjacency, and a 90% interval on every number. The tier is inferred from the input:

| Tier | Input | Phone |
|---|---|---|
| LiDAR | Stray Scanner recording: depth, confidence, poses, intrinsics | iPhone 12 Pro or newer Pro |
| Video | one Camera-app walkthrough (RGB only) | any iPhone 15 or newer |
| Photos | a folder per room of stills (RGB only) | any iPhone 15 or newer |

Damage (§8) runs on all three tiers, online (Gemini) or offline (Florence-2); the staged
hole's size is inside the interval on all 7 runs. Not built: window detection, the photo
tier's stitch into one plan.

## 2. Architecture: one geometry backend for every tier

Every tier is converted into the same thing, a **metric, z-up point cloud with camera
positions**, and one backend measures it (`src/floorplan/lidar.build_plan`):

```
LiDAR   depth + poses ──── cloud.fuse ─────────┐
Video   frames ── Pi3X (chained windows) ──────┼─> levels -> plan2d -> rooms -> openings -> plan.json
Photos  per-room stills ── Pi3X per room ──────┘   floor/    walls,     outline,   doors,
        (scale bias divided out, intervals.py)      ceiling   rooms      wall faces passages
```

- **Levels** (`levels.py`): floor and ceiling from height histograms; a ceiling peak must
  stand apart from stronger peaks below it (fix 02).
- **Walls and rooms** (`plan2d.py`): a cell is wall when its points span ≥ 0.8 m of height
  (counter fronts, capped by a hollow-under top, are not walls); free space enclosed by
  walls (ray test) is split into rooms at narrow passages and under door lintels; floor
  seen past an unscanned wall is kept as a flagged low-confidence zone (fix 01c).
- **Room outline** (`rooms.py`): a rectilinear polygon of each room's free space; each edge
  is then moved onto its wall. Where the ceiling was seen, the wall is the **median of the
  points 2.3 m to 0.3 m under the ceiling** behind the edge, own side of interior walls
  (fix 09, §6); elsewhere the nearest wall face.
- **Openings** (`openings.py`): gaps at body height in each wall's elevation, confirmed by
  seeing through them; door if a lintel is above, passage if open to the ceiling.
- Video longer than 30 frames is reconstructed in overlapping windows of 30 chained by
  similarity fits on shared frames; a broken link is refitted on the shared frames that
  agree (`chunks.fit_shared`).

**Models** (§10): the video and photo tiers use Pi3X (default) or MapAnything
(`--model mapanything`, Apache 2.0) for multi-view reconstruction, chosen in a bake-off of
three models on home C ([model_research.md](model_research.md), [photo_tier.md](photo_tier.md)).
Each model places surfaces too close to the cameras by a stable factor (Pi3X 0.89
horizontally, 0.91 vertically), which is divided out (`intervals.SCALE_BIAS`,
[video_scale.md](video_scale.md)).

## 5. Drift

ARKit poses drift: a wall seen early and late lands in two places. We measured it first
(`scripts/drift_probe.py`: revisited walls move up to 8.8 cm in `c_home_01`) and built a
correction (`drift.py`, `--drift`): frames in chunks of 150, each chunk's wall points
registered top-down to the earlier chunks by trimmed 2D ICP (yaw + shift), accepted only
with ≥ 50% matched points, both wall directions constrained, |yaw| < 3°, shift < 0.3 m.
Ablation on all 9 captures ([drift_ablation.md](drift_ablation.md)): it made the damage
room's repeat scans agree (21 → 1 cm) but made A's `c00a170fe1` and C's kitchen worse, so
it is **off by default** and the multi-room plans use the poses as recorded.

What does handle drift by default is the wall estimate: drift smears a wall into a band
10–40 cm thick, and since fix 09 each wall is placed at the band's centre (median), not at
its inner edge. That took the damage room's two scans from 21 cm to 0.4 cm apart on the
short side, with drift correction off.

## 6. The fix loop (declaration: [fix_declaration.md](fix_declaration.md))

**Failing number.** On home C every LiDAR room side was short: median 29 cm, worst −53 cm,
areas −11 to −30%, the true area outside our interval in 6 of 6 rooms; Polycam 6 cm.

**Root cause** (`scripts/diag_walls.py`, per wall): everything through our plan builder was
short on all three tiers and every model; only SpatialLM, with its own wall finder, was not.
In the cloud, each wall is a dense band of points up to 40 cm thick (drift) and the old
snap took its inner edge; furniture (kitchen wall cabinets 1 ft deep, wardrobes) counted as
wall. Drift correction did not remove the bias (checked). Four rules were compared offline
before choosing.

**Fix.** Measure each wall only above furniture and door heads (2.3 m to 0.3 m under the
ceiling), at the median of its points, own side of interior walls; elsewhere unchanged.

**Predicted vs measured.** Predicted per room from the offline run, ±3 cm: every LiDAR room
landed within 3 cm of its prediction. Median 29 → **5 cm**; truth inside the area interval
0 → 3 of 6; head-to-head 0 → 6 of 8. **One prediction was wrong**: the video tier did not
move. Its scale correction had been fitted against our short LiDAR, copying its error. A
declared follow-up re-measured it (Pi3X 0.964 → 0.890); the damage-room clips then landed as
predicted (+5 / +6 cm), in-sample. Before and after: tags `fixloop-before`, `fixloop-after`.

**Short of the gate.** The repeatability gate (1 cm / 0.5%) still fails on the bedroom's long
side (4.6 cm) and across B's scans, whose outlines differ before any wall placement (B's
coverage differs per scan). The dining room's short side is 23 cm short: its west wall has
no points at any height (§9).

## 7. Error budget and calibration

Intervals are 90% (±1.645 σ). Per wall, σ is the spread of the points the wall was placed
from (the drift smear's 1.4826 × MAD where it was measured high up, the face spread
elsewhere); a wall length combines the two walls at its ends; an area combines all walls.
Video and photo add, in quadrature, the scale residual (Pi3X: 5% horizontal, 2% vertical
for video; 14% / 5.5% for photos, the 90th percentile of the photo tier's error on home C
divided by 1.645, fitted on C itself) and 5 cm of surface error.

| Source (LiDAR, home C) | Size |
|---|---|
| Drift smear of a wall (σ of the band) | 2–15 cm per wall |
| Wall placement vs truth after fix 09 | median 5 cm per side |
| Ceiling | ±1 cm (3 scans of one room agree within 1.3 cm) |
| Design vs built (drawing as truth) | a few cm, unknown |
| Unscanned wall | unbounded on that side (−23 cm seen) |

Empirical coverage of the 90% intervals (`uv run scripts/calibration.py`): room sides
inside for LiDAR 10 of 11, video 12 of 13, photos 12 of 15; ceilings 4 of 4, 6 of 6, 7 of 8. Areas are compared with the
drawing's nominal rectangles (closet and niche not subtracted), so their 3 of 6 is
pessimistic. The photo misses are rooms whose photos covered only part of the room (a
third too small): the interval was widened to the tier's measured error, but a room seen
in part can still fall outside it. That is the thin-input risk the brief caps; the
pipeline warns when a folder has fewer than 4 photos.

## 8. Damage detection and the choice of model

**Pipeline** (`src/floorplan/damage*.py`, research in [damage_research.md](damage_research.md)).
A hosted vision model finds and labels damage on each image as boxes: crack, water stain,
mould, hole, peeling paint, plus sockets and exposed wiring as context for the rules. The
model never measures anything. Each box is outlined in the image without a model
(contrast against the surrounding wall, ellipse fallback), and the outline is cast through
the frame's pose onto the measured wall plane. That gives area and position in wall
coordinates (along the wall, height above the floor). The 90% interval is a Monte Carlo
over the outline (±2 px), the wall's position, the pose and the floor height. A damage
seen in several frames is merged, and its draws are pooled. On the LiDAR tier, a box where
the depth shows an object in front of the wall (a picture frame) is dropped. Rules
R1–R5 and R7–R10 raise concealed-damage flags that name their rule; scope items are
Xactimate-style (trade code, surface id, quantity with interval, no pricing). Every model
answer is cached in `data/damage_cache/`, so the numbers replay offline.

**Model choice.** Three Gemini models on the 26 photos of the staged bedroom, scored image
by image against labels drawn by eye and corrected by the user
([damage_models.md](damage_models.md), `uv run scripts/damage_compare.py`):

| | **Gemini 3.1 Pro** (chosen) | Gemini 3.8 Flash | Gemini 3.6 Flash |
|---|---|---|---|
| Holes found (10 photos) | **10 / 10** | 9 / 10 | 9 / 10 |
| False holes | **0** | 2 | 0 |
| Exposed wiring found (7 photos) | 7 / 7 | 7 / 7 | 7 / 7 |
| Damage-free photos left clean | **16 / 16** | 12 / 16 | 11 / 16 |
| Touch grime called water stain | **2 photos** | 5 photos | 5 photos |
| Seconds per photo (2 calls) | **8** | 24 | 14 |

3.1 Pro is the default because it is best on every row: no missed hole, no false damage on
a clean photo, and the fastest. A false alarm costs more here than a missed blemish: each
one becomes a flag and a scope line a homeowner pays to check. Speed matters at the
walk-in. Gemini 2.5 Flash, the first model tried, also called grime marks water stains.
`FLOORPLAN_GEMINI_MODEL` switches models; each model's answers are cached separately.

**Sizing check** ([damage_hole.md](damage_hole.md), `scripts/damage_hole_check.py --md`;
the friend's tape: 6.35 cm hole = 31.7 cm², centre 1.420 m up, 2.286 m from the right-hand
wall). All 7 runs find it and all 7 intervals hold the truth: LiDAR 30.4 / 37.0 cm²
(Gemini) and 30.4 / 32.9 (Florence-2 offline), video 32.9 / 28.5 (60 frames), photo 28.0
(8 photos). Height within 3 cm except photo (−11 cm); along the wall LiDAR is +11 to +13 cm
on both scans while video is +3 / +7 cm, so the bias is in our LiDAR corner, not the tape.

**Video and photo tiers** detect on the images the reconstruction used (video: its even
frames; photos: every photo) and size from the model's per-pixel points, projected onto
the wall; intervals include the tier's scale error. **Offline fallback:** Florence-2-base
on the laptop (`FLOORPLAN_DAMAGE_DETECTOR=florence`, automatic when Gemini fails after
retries). It grounds any phrase to the most salient thing, so it only proposes boxes; a
caption of each crop gives the class. On the 26 photos it finds 4 of 10 hole photos
(misses far ones) at 3 s per photo.

**Limits.** Every model calls grime from hands on a white wall "water stain"; a prompt
change would likely fix it but would be tuned on this same test set, so it is not made. A
second crack-only prompt (`--crack-pass`, traced polylines) is experimental: Pro once
looped 54 segments on a clean wall (now discarded), and it traces the outline of a plaster
patch as a crack. The labels are image-level and only 26 photos of one room, so these are
indications, not accuracy figures. Pause-based frame choice is built for LiDAR only (ROADMAP).

## 9. Known failure modes

- **Unscanned walls.** A wall with no points is placed at the edge of the floor seen; the
  room is short on that side (dining room −23 cm). The floor past it is flagged
  low-confidence and the area interval widened. Only a rescan fixes it; the protocol asks
  to sweep every wall top to bottom.
- **Mirrors and glass.** LiDAR and the models see the reflection as space behind the wall
  (a mirror is a doorway); glass reads as a surface at the glass or the frame. The protocol
  asks to cover large mirrors. Windows are not detected for this reason.
- **Wet-look, black and very shiny surfaces** return little LiDAR depth (confidence < 2 is
  dropped), leaving holes like an unscanned wall.
- **Low light** blurs video frames and raises LiDAR noise; the protocol asks for all lights
  on. Not measured.
- **Low scans.** If the phone never points above ~2.3 m (A's `c00a170fe1` stops at 1.7 m),
  walls fall back to the nearest face and furniture can count as wall.
- **Long videos.** A 5-minute whole-house Camera-app clip with ceiling sweeps folds rooms
  onto each other in the chained reconstruction; room-sized clips (60 frames) work.
- **Photo stitch.** Per-room folders are reconstructed separately and laid side by side,
  not stitched; few photos per room give partial rooms (gate G7 not met).
- **Door widths** come out narrow (open door leaf and frame in the gap).
- **Scale bias varies by room** (Pi3X 0.80–0.99 horizontally): ±10% per room on the video
  and photo tiers, covered by their wider intervals.

## 10. Disclosure: models, data, compute

- **Pi3X** (yyfz/Pi3, weights CC BY-NC 4.0: non-commercial) is the default image model;
  **MapAnything** (Meta, Apache 2.0 weights) is the commercial-use option. Depth Anything 3
  and SpatialLM 1.1 (Qwen-0.5B, Llama-1B) were tested and not adopted
  ([lidar_model_research.md](lidar_model_research.md)).
- **Gemini 3.1 Pro (Google, hosted API on Vertex AI)** detects damage (§8): images are sent
  to Google; answers are cached in `data/damage_cache/`. Allowed by Cozmo with disclosure.
  Sizes, flags and scope are computed locally.
- **Datasets:** ARKitScenes (Apple, research licence) for B; Cozmo's captures for A; C is
  our own capture with the owner's permission.
- **Compute:** the image model runs on Kaggle (two T4s, fp16): by default a video or photo
  run uploads its frames to a private Kaggle dataset and runs one GPU job (~4-5 min), then
  caches the result (`FLOORPLAN_RECON_CACHE`), which is how every number here regenerates
  without a GPU. Without network, a low-memory local mode runs small windows in separate
  processes; Pi3X does not fit this 16 GB laptop, so that mode is for larger machines.
- **Florence-2-base** (Microsoft, MIT) is the offline damage detector on the laptop.
- **Competitor:** Polycam 7.0.4 (Apple RoomPlan) on the same home; screenshots of its plan,
  numbers transcribed into [data/gt/home_c_polycam.json](../data/gt/home_c_polycam.json).
